# About Maskan

**Maskan** (Arabic for *residence*) is an AI property concierge built as a technical demo. It answers questions about:

- **DarGlobal's 38 projects** across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Spain and the UK
- **Wasalt: 957 listings for sale and 16 projects** (villas and apartments) in **Riyadh and Jeddah**

**How it works.** An AI agent chooses its own tools for each question: exact database filters for prices, sizes and bedrooms; hybrid semantic + keyword search for descriptions and amenities; and calculators for price per sqm, mortgages and Gulf currency conversion. Open any step in a reply to see what it called and what came back. Every listing and project is linked to its source page.
